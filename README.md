# Doctor X: Mafia in White (2026)

## A. Introduction
Doctor X: Mafia in White is a Korean medical drama series about a brilliant surgeon who is not afraid to stand up against powerful people in the hospital. The story shows how doctors face difficult situations while trying to save their patients. It also shows the problems of corruption, unfair treatment, and the importance of putting patients first than everything.

Doctor X: Mafia in White is a South Korean medical drama series released in 2026. It belongs to the medical drama genre and is presented in the Korean language.

## B. Synopsis
Doctor X: Mafia in White follows the story of Gye Su-jeong, a very talented and intelligent surgeon who joins Guseo University Hospital. She is known for her excellent skills and her ability to handle difficult operations that other doctors may not want to perform. However, she discovers that the hospital is full of powerful people who care more about their positions and personal interests than their patients. Because of this, she faces many challenges and conflicts with other hospital staff. With the help of some people around her, she continues to save lives and stand up against unfair actions. Throughout the story, she proves that being a good doctor is not only about having great skills but also about having courage and caring for others.

## C. Characters
1. Gye Su-jeong (Kim Ji-won) – She is the main character and a very talented surgeon.

2. Jang Hui-suk (Lee Jung-eun) – She runs a medical placement agency and helps Su-jeong get a position at the hospital. 

3. Bu Seung-gwon (Son Hyun-joo) – He is the director of Guseo University Hospital. 

4. Park Tae-gyeong (Kim Woo-seok) – He is a surgical intern in hospital, He is kind, caring, and looks up to Su-jeong.

5. Jo Won-jin (Lee Jin-hee) – She is an experienced anesthesiology professor at the hospital.
